Elevator maintenance and AMC in Port Blair

An Annual Maintenance Contract keeps your lift safe, predictable and compliant. Planned visits catch wear before it becomes a breakdown.

Preventive maintenance

Built for island conditions.

Scheduled servicing

Visits agreed in advance, monthly, bi-monthly or quarterly depending on how heavily the lift is used. Each visit ends with a written service note.

Inspections

Doors, safety devices, controller, drive and machine are checked. Faults found early come with a clear plan and price.

Breakdown support

AMC customers are first in line for breakdown calls. A technician is dispatched from Port Blair.

Spare-parts coordination

We identify the parts needed, arrange them with suppliers, and confirm the cost before anything is fitted. Island shipping times are planned for.

Monsoon checks

What gets checked before and during the rains.

Most island lift failures start with moisture or power. Our visits are built to catch both early.

  • Cleaning and lubrication of guides, ropes or belts, and door tracks
  • Brake, levelling and ride-quality checks
  • Safety gear, limit switches and door interlocks tested
  • Emergency phone and alarm checked
  • Contacts, relays and control panels inspected for salt and moisture damage
  • Earthing and supply checks ahead of the monsoon

How an AMC works

Three steps to a predictable lift.

  1. Assessment of your lift

    We inspect the existing lift, note its condition, and flag anything needing repair before the contract starts.

  2. Agreed scope and schedule

    You receive a written contract with the visit frequency, what is covered, and what is billed separately.

  3. Visits, reports and approvals

    Each visit is logged. Spares are never fitted without your approval.

Who AMC suits

Owners who want fewer surprises.

  • Apartment associations and property managers
  • Hotels and resorts with guests depending on the lift
  • Offices and institutional buildings
  • Owners of existing lifts looking for reliable upkeep
