About us

A new lift company, rooted in Port Blair

Nicoman Elevators is a new elevator solutions company focused on reliable installation, maintenance, repair and servicing. We work mainly with G+3 and G+4 buildings, hotels and other suitable properties.

Talk to us

Our vision

The local lift service Andaman buildings call first.

We want to be the company that buildings keep calling for years after handover, not only on installation day.

Our service philosophy

Safety comes first. Quality is built into every connection, not inspected in afterwards. Reliability is measured by how seldom a lift stops. When work has to be done, we do it on time and tell you when.

Our commitments

Safety, quality, reliability, timely execution.

Safety

Safety circuits and emergency systems are tested at installation, at handover and on every service visit.

Quality

Parts, wiring and workmanship are checked before we sign off, not after a complaint.

Reliability

Planned maintenance and clear fault reports, so you always know what was done and why.

Timely execution

Agreed timelines for installation and visits. If a date slips, you hear it from us first.

Local presence

We work where you do.

Our base is Port Blair. Our technicians understand island realities: salt air that corrodes contacts, monsoon power dips, and spare parts that take longer to arrive. We plan for all of them.
