Solutions

Commercial

Step one

Site inspection and requirement assessment.

Before any quote, we visit the site and measure what matters. Lift projects go wrong when details are found too late, so we check them first.

  • Shaft width, depth and height, plus pit depth and drainage
  • Overhead clearance and roof access
  • Machine-room or machine-room-less layout
  • Floors, stops and entrance positions
  • Load, cabin size and speed needs
  • Power supply, earthing and where the panel will sit
  • Route for carrying materials into the building

Technical specifications

Specifications, explained plainly.

Drive

Variable-voltage, variable-frequency (VVVF) drives give smooth starts and stops, and lower energy use.

Machine

Machine-room or machine-room-less options. We recommend one based on your building, not a catalogue default.

Controls

Microprocessor controller with safety circuits, emergency phone and alarm, and clear fault codes for the technician.

Standards

Designed and tested to applicable Indian lift standards, including the IS 14665 series, with inspection support for your statutory approval.

Final specifications are confirmed in writing in your proposal.

Installation process

From first call to handover.

  1. Enquiry and call-back

    Tell us your location, building type, floors and the service you need.

  2. Site inspection

    We measure the shaft and check power, access and structure.

  3. Proposal and quotation

    A written proposal with specifications, scope, timeline and price.

  4. Civil readiness with your builder

    We share a checklist: shaft finishing, pit, machine room, power point, lighting and lobby openings.

  5. Installation

    Guide rails, car, counterweight, machine, controller and doors are fitted and aligned.

  6. Testing, inspection and handover

    Safety and load tests, documentation, and a walkthrough for owners and staff. An AMC can start from handover.
