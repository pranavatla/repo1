Fault diagnosis

We find the fault before we fix it.

  1. Listen to the symptom

    We ask what happened, when it started, and whether there was a power cut or storm.

  2. Read the controller

    Fault codes, error logs and the state of each safety circuit.

  3. Isolate and test

    Doors, drive, brake, sensors and wiring are tested one at a time, not guessed at.

  4. Explain and quote

    You get the fault in plain words and a price before repair work starts.

Repair and servicing

What we repair and service.

Mechanical repairs

Door rollers and guide shoes, ropes or belts, brakes, levelling, and motor or gearbox issues.

Electrical repairs

Controller boards, contactors and relays, sensors and limit switches, wiring and earth faults after storms.

Door-system servicing

Door operators, interlocks, door sensors, track alignment and sill cleaning.

Control-system servicing

Call buttons, floor indicators, position sensing, and emergency phone and alarm checks.

Before you call

What to tell us.

A few details help us bring the right parts and tools on the first visit.

If someone is trapped, call 112 first, then call us. Do not try to open the doors or move the car yourself.
  • Which floor is the car on, or is it stuck between floors?
  • Are the doors opening, closing, or stuck?
  • Is there an error code or flashing light on the controller or panel?
  • When did it start, and was there a power cut or storm recently?
  • Is the lift used by guests, residents or staff right now?
