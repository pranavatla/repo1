Why choose Nicoman

Built for island conditions, not just brochures.

Salt air, monsoon humidity and power that dips during storms wear lifts out faster than on the mainland. We plan for all three.

Salt air and humidity

Salt and moisture corrode contacts, control panels and door tracks. We inspect and clean those parts on every visit.

Monsoon power

Voltage dips and surges during storms damage controllers. We check earthing, supply quality and protection before they cause a breakdown.

Spares planned ahead

Parts for island sites take longer to arrive. We identify what is likely to be needed during survey and AMC visits, so downtime stays short.

Local presence

We are based in Port Blair, so our technicians reach your building without waiting on a mainland team.

Safety before handover

Installations and repairs are checked against applicable Indian lift standards before we sign them off.
